# Cover art credits

The pictures behind the covers in `images/covers.jpg` are public-domain works from Wikimedia Commons (the licence is shown on each file page). The covers themselves were laid out by the app's Cover tool.

- **The Time Machine**: *The Course of Empire: Desolation*, Thomas Cole, 1836. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cole_Thomas_The_Course_of_Empire_Desolation_1836.jpg (public domain)
- **The Island of Doctor Moreau**: *Isle of the Dead (third version)*, Arnold Böcklin, 1883. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Arnold_B%C3%B6cklin_-_Die_Toteninsel_III_%28Alte_Nationalgalerie%2C_Berlin%29.jpg (public domain)
- **Narrative of the Life of Frederick Douglass**: *Frederick Douglass, daguerreotype portrait*, Samuel J. Miller, 1847–52. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Frederick_Douglass_by_Samuel_J_Miller%2C_1847-52.png (public domain)
- **The War of the Worlds**: *The War of the Worlds, original graphic (tripod and sunken ship)*, Henrique Alvim Corrêa, 1906. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_War_of_the_Worlds_by_Henrique_Alvim_Corr%C3%AAa%2C_original_graphic_15.jpg (public domain)
- **Frankenstein**: *The Sea of Ice (Das Eismeer)*, Caspar David Friedrich, 1823–24. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Caspar_David_Friedrich_-_Das_Eismeer_-_Hamburger_Kunsthalle_-_02.jpg (public domain)
- **Up from Slavery**: *Roof construction by students at Tuskegee Institute*, Frances Benjamin Johnston, c. 1902. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Roof_construction_by_students_at_Tuskegee_Institute.jpg (public domain)
- **Twelve Years a Slave**: *A Cotton Plantation on the Mississippi*, Currier & Ives, 1884. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:A_cotton_plantation_on_the_Mississippi_LCCN91722891.tif (public domain)
- **Personal Memoirs of U. S. Grant**: *General Robert E. Lee surrenders at Appomattox Court House, 1865*, Thomas Nast, c. 1895. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:General_Robert_E._Lee_surrenders_at_Appomattox_Court_House_1865.jpg (public domain)
